[Tableau Project Publication](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/sampson.okereke/viz/DigitalMarketingPerformance_17664338977580/ExecutiveDashboard#1)
- **The problem:** Marketing leadership needed a single source of truth for full-funnel digital performance across channels, campaigns, and creative formats — without waiting on manual reports or digging through disconnected spreadsheets.
- Built a 3-dashboard Tableau analytics suite covering the full decision-making loop: an Executive Summary for leadership, a Channel & Budget Optimization view for reallocation decisions, and an Ad Type & Creative Performance dashboard for creative strategy.
- The Executive Dashboard surfaces KPI scorecards, weekly spend and efficiency trendlines, and channel comparisons — designed to answer "what's driving results and what's changing?" in under 60 seconds.
- The Channel & Budget Optimization view identifies efficiency gaps by comparing conversion share vs. spend share, surfacing channels that are over-indexed on spend relative to performance and flagging reallocation opportunities.
- The Creative Performance dashboard ranks ad formats by funnel KPIs — awareness, engagement, and conversion — with best/2nd-best callout cards to directly inform decisions about which formats to scale.
- Implemented reusable calculated fields standardizing all KPIs (CTR, CVR, CPA, view rate, completion rate, engagement rate, spend share, conversion share) with fast filtering by date, campaign, channel, ad type, and audience.

### Bank Loan Reports - SQL Project 
[Project Publication](https://github.com/sampsoncco/Sampson-Okereke-SQL-Portfolio-/blob/main/Bank%20Loan%20Reports.sql)

- **The problem:** A bank needed structured reporting on its borrower portfolio — segmented by loan characteristics, payment behavior, and risk profile — from a raw database with no pre-built views.
- Wrote a suite of complex SQL reports using CTEs, multi-table JOINs, CONCAT functions, DATEPART functions, and aggregations to transform raw loan records into clean, decision-ready output.
- Structured queries for reusability and clarity — modular CTEs with clearly scoped logic, consistent aliasing, and aggregations that can be adapted to new segmentation needs without rewriting from scratch.
- Segmented borrower data by multiple characteristics simultaneously, producing reports that give lending teams a full picture of portfolio risk, repayment patterns, and borrower demographics in a single query run.
